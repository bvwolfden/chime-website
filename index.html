See everyone at a glance

One map for the people you love

Clann puts your whole family on a single, always-current map. Glance once and you'll know who's home, who's on the move, who might need a charger soon — and whose phone is locked or limited right now.

  • Real-time positions — everyone's latest location, right where you'd expect it.
  • Battery at a glance — spot a low phone before it goes dark.
  • Screen-time status on the roster — locked, limited, or free, without opening anything.
  • Sharing on your terms — every member controls their own visibility.
New · Usage

Screen time you can actually see

Every kid's day at a glance, and every week in perspective. Clann shows the time, the apps behind it, and whether it's rising or falling — so the conversation starts with facts instead of guesses.

  • Today, per kid — total time, pickups, longest session, top apps, and the nearest limit.
  • Hour by hour — across school, free time, and sleep, with what stayed blocked.
  • The whole week — when they're on the phone, compared to their usual, rising or falling.
Healthy screen time, together

Balance without the battles

Set the rules once and Clann helps them hold — app and category budgets, a sleep block, school hours, and a calm block screen that tells your kid why and for how long. When they ask for more, you decide with a single tap.

  • App & category limits — daily budgets for Social, Games, Entertainment, or one specific app.
  • Bedtime & school hours — quiet hours that wind the phone down automatically, at home or at school.
  • Bonus time, scoped — unlock just the app or category they asked for, not the whole phone.
  • Lock all, one tap — dinner, homework, or bedtime for every kid's phone at once, and you see it land.

Peace of mind on the road

Not just where. How.

For the newest drivers and the farthest trips, Clann quietly keeps watch — live speed and altitude while they're moving, an arrival note when they're safe, and a replay of the whole trip you can talk through together.

  • Live telemetry — speed, altitude, and battery, plus one tap to know the moment they leave.
  • Rewind any trip — route, speed, and every stop, played back exactly as it happened.
  • Even at 35,000 feet — in the car, on the water, in the air, Clann keeps up.
  • Find them, then drive to them — Precision Find for the last few feet, turn-by-turn to a family member on the move.

Private by design, always

Clann exists to bring your family closer — not to sell what it learns. Your family's data stays within your family. No advertising, no data brokers, no cross-app tracking — and we never sell your data. Screen-time detail comes from Apple's Family Controls and stays on your family's devices.
