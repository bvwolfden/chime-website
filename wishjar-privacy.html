Mom's Wish Jar

Privacy Policy — Last updated May 7, 2026

Mom's Wish Jar is a private family app built as a personal gift. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

What We Collect

  • Name — entered by each family member during setup
  • Role — Mom or Family member
  • Wish text — the wishes Mom creates each day
  • Device push token — used only to deliver notifications within the family

Where Data Is Stored

All data is stored in Apple CloudKit (iCloud), using Apple's infrastructure. We do not operate any external servers. Data is tied to your iCloud account and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

How Data Is Used

  • To display Mom's wishes to family members
  • To send push notifications when a wish is created or claimed
  • To maintain a wish history as a personal family keepsake

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell or share your data with any third party
  • We do not use analytics or advertising services
  • We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data beyond what is listed above

Data Deletion

To delete your data, sign out of iCloud or contact us at bvukmir@gmail.com and we will remove your records from CloudKit.

Children

This app is designed for family use including children. No data from any user is shared outside the family or used for any commercial purpose.
