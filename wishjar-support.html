Mom's Wish Jar

Support — Last updated May 11, 2026

Mom's Wish Jar is a family app designed to make Mother's Day — and every day — a little easier for Mom. This page provides answers to common questions and a way to get help.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

  • The person setting up the family taps Set Up, enters details, and generates a 6-character join code.
  • Mom and other family members tap I'm Mom or Join Family and enter the code.
  • Once joined, Mom can create one wish per day. Family members see and claim it.

Why can't I sign in?

Mom's Wish Jar uses Apple iCloud (CloudKit) for data sync. Make sure:

  • You're signed into iCloud in your iPhone Settings
  • You have an internet connection
  • You're entering the join code correctly (codes are case-insensitive)

How do I delete my account?

In the app, tap the Settings icon, then Delete Account. This will remove your profile and any wishes you've created from the family's shared data. The deletion is permanent.

Can Mom make more than one wish a day?

No — the app intentionally limits Mom to one wish per day. The whole idea is to reduce her mental load, not add to it.

Why are wishes written in cursive?

Wishes are written in a flowing cursive font (Dancing Script) to feel personal and handwritten — like a small love note rather than a task list.

What happens to my data?

All data is stored in Apple CloudKit (iCloud) and is accessible only to members of your family. We do not collect analytics, share data with third parties, or use any external servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.
