Getting started

How do I set up Clann for my family?

Setup takes just a few minutes:

  • Download Clann on the parent's iPhone and sign in with your Apple Account.
  • Create your family, then send an invite to each family member.
  • On each person's phone, open the invite to join and choose their location-sharing preference.
  • For a child's device, follow the on-screen prompts to enable location and (optionally) Screen Time controls.

Once everyone has joined, you'll see the whole family on your map.

How do family invites work?

From the app, tap to invite a family member and share the link or code with them. When they open it on their iPhone, Clann connects their device to your family automatically. Invites are private to your family and can be managed from the app.

Which devices does Clann support?

Clann is built for iPhone. Location sharing and parental controls rely on Apple frameworks that are available on modern versions of iOS. For the best experience, keep everyone's device on a recent iOS version.

Location & places

Why does Clann need "Always" location access?

To show live location and notify your family when someone arrives at or leaves a place, iOS requires background ("Always") location permission. Without it, locations only update while the app is open, and arrival alerts won't fire reliably.

You can review or change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Clann.

Someone's location looks old or isn't updating

A stale location is almost always a device or permission issue on that person's phone. Check that, on their device:

  • Clann's location permission is set to Always and Precise Location is on.
  • Location Services is enabled and the phone has signal and battery.
  • They're actively sharing their location in the app.

Opening Clann on their phone will refresh their position right away.

How do I add a place like home or school?

Open the Places tab, add a location, and give it a name. Clann will let the family know when someone arrives at or leaves that place. You can adjust the area for each place so alerts trigger where you want them.

Can I stop sharing my location?

Yes. Every family member controls their own sharing and can turn it off at any time. When you're not sharing, your location isn't collected, and your family will see that you're not currently sharing.

Screen Time & parental controls

How do I set screen-time limits for my child?

On the parent device, open your child's controls and set app or category limits, bedtime, and downtime. On the child's device, you'll approve Apple's Screen Time permission once (Family Controls). After that, Clann applies the limits and shows a calm block screen when one is reached.

My child asked for more time — how do I approve it?

When your child taps "ask for more time," you'll get a notification. You can approve a set amount right from the notification or the app — no need to be on their device. The extra time applies to their phone within moments.

The block screen or a limit isn't behaving as expected

Make sure the child's device has completed the Screen Time (Family Controls) setup and is signed in to iCloud with the child's own Apple Account. If usage or limits look off after restoring a phone from backup, re-check that iCloud sign-in — it's the most common cause. Still stuck? Email support@prvail.ai and describe what you're seeing.

Account & privacy

Is my family's data private?

Yes. Your family's location and controls are shared only within your family. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps. Read the full Privacy Policy for details, including the short list of service providers we use.

How do I delete my account or data?

You can remove family members and delete your account from within the app. Deleting your account removes your associated personal data (aside from limited records we may be legally required to keep). Prefer we handle it? Email support@prvail.ai.

How do I leave a family?

Open the app, go to your family settings, and choose to leave. Once you leave, you'll no longer share location with that family or appear on their map.
